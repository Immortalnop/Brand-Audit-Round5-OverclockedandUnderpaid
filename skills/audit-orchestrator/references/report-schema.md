# Report schema

Top-level: site, audited_at, summary (status_reason first when no page could
be analyzed, then total_findings, critical, high, medium,
low, proactive_improvements, insufficient_evidence_items), coverage
(pages_discovered, pages_analyzed, pages_skipped, rendered_pages,
external_claims_checked, external_footprint_queries, external_fetches,
identity_confidence, memory_budget_used_mb, runtime_seconds,
deadline_reached), warnings, findings (see finding-schema.md),
recommendations, whats_working.

Recommendations come in two shapes. A proactive finding is linked, not
copied: `{"id": "R-...", "finding_id": "F-...", "check_id", "check_ids",
"category", "finding_type": "proactive_improvement", "title", "status",
"provenance": {"source": "finding_reference"}}`. Its evidence, steps, action
plan and snippet are on the finding it names. Validation drops a link whose
finding is not in the report. The other recommendations are baseline
maintenance items for categories with no finding; they have the full finding
fields and their own `suggested_action`.

`whats_working` lists the checks that ran and passed, in a fixed order:
`{"id": "W-...", "check", "title", "check_ids", "evidence", "pages_checked"}`.
A check is listed only when every skill that runs it finished with status
success, no finding or recommendation carries any of its `check_ids`, and the
observation in `evidence` holds on the snapshot (for example, every page
checked has a title). A check with nothing to look at is not listed.
`pages_checked` is null for robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt checks. When no
page was analyzed only those three kinds can appear; an audit that did not
finish has an empty list. Implemented in
`skills/audit-orchestrator/scripts/whats_working.py`.


Coverage also reports `offer_expiry` (`offer_expiry_pages_scanned`,
`offer_deadlines_found`, `expired_offer_pages`, `offer_expiry_reference_date`),
`fact_consistency` (`facts_compared_with_text`, `markup_text_mismatches`,
`cross_page_conflicts`, `fact_consistency_pages`) and `ai_crawler_purposes`
(crawler name to `search`, `training`, `user_fetch` or `unclassified`, from
common/ai_crawlers.py).

Network allowance: `coverage.network_budget` is the shared ledger every
transport of the audit charged (common/network_budget.py): `pools.site` (the
audited origin, a re-anchored origin, robots.txt, retries, redirect hops, the
HTTPS probe, media, llms.txt link checks and the headless browser's reserved
slice) and `pools.external` (knowledge graphs, linked profiles, the PageSpeed
API and a configured search provider), each `{"max_requests", "max_bytes",
"requests", "bytes", "refused"}`, plus `stages` (requests and bytes per stage
label), `total_requests`, `total_bytes`, `accounting` and `not_counted`. A
request is reserved before it is sent and counted even when it fails; bytes are
the larger of wire and decoded bytes, and unused reserved bytes are refunded.
The headless browser runs in a child process and is charged its whole slice.
Host-agent tool calls are not counted. `--max-pages` raises the site pool, and
the raised ceiling is what `max_requests` reports.

Time plan: `timing` is added by the audit runner: `workflow_target_s` (270),
`core_limit_s` (the enforced worker limit, 270 by default), `agent_stage_budget_s` (the cap, 55),
`finalization_budget_s`, `session_started_at`, `agent_deadline_at`,
`session_deadline_at` (UTC), `core_elapsed_s`, `agent_time_left_s`,
`agent_stage_recommended` and `enforcement`. Only the core limit is enforced by
this code; the agent stage runs in the host and its deadline is advisory. The
merge records `coverage.agent_corroboration.workflow_timing`: the measured time
from the audit start to the merge and whether it met the target.

Audit binding: every report carries `audit_id` (generated by the audit runner),
`snapshot_sha256` (SHA-256 over the acquired observations: page identities,
statuses, titles and a SHA-256 of each page's extracted visible text, not raw
response bytes; `null` when the worker did not finish) and `code_version`
(`<release>+<commit>`). `--evidence-out` writes the agent evidence packet with
the same `audit_id` and `snapshot_sha256` (fields and limits in
`common/agent_evidence.py`: at most 512 KiB, 8 pages, 4,000 characters per page
and 12 claims; omissions and truncation are listed in its `limitations`).

Agent stage: `coverage.agent_stage` is `{"submission_status", "assessment_status",
"checks_assessed", "checks_not_assessed", "assessed_count", "not_assessed_count",
"checks_with_errors", "checks"}`. `submission_status` is `not_supplied`,
`legacy` or `envelope` (what was received); `assessment_status` is `not_run`,
`partial` or `complete` (how many of the six checks were actually assessed, a
pass or a finding under the envelope's evidence contract). `checks` holds one
record per AG check: `outcome` (`pass`, `finding`, `not_assessed` or `error`),
`reason`, and where present `source_refs`, `queries`, `severity`, `status` and
`severity_capped`. `coverage.agent_checks_assessed` lists the assessed checks.
`coverage.agent_corroboration_stage` summarises it: `not_supplied`,
`legacy_partial` (a legacy findings array, which assesses no check), or the
envelope's `assessment_status`. Reports written before this field used
`ingested`.

A merged report also carries `coverage.agent_corroboration`: `{"merged_from",
"evidence_packet", "submission", "merged_at", "findings_supplied",
"findings_ingested", "findings_folded_into_existing", "findings_added"}`. An
agent finding that reports the same check as a script finding is folded into it
(its evidence, pages, `check_ids` and provenance are added; severity never
drops); an envelope finding that names pages joins only a finding sharing one
of them. The others are appended. Envelope evidence items keep their packet
`evidence_id` through renumbering. The merge renumbers findings, rebuilds
`recommendations`, `top_actions`, `quick_wins` and the summary, removes the
not-supplied warning, clears only the limitations of checks actually assessed,
adds a limitation for each check not assessed, and keeps `confidence`, fix
snippets and the `whats_working` entries no merged finding contradicts.
Severity ceilings: AG-01, AG-04 and AG-05 medium; AG-02 and AG-06 low; AG-03
high only for a material conflict with an inspected source, else medium; no
agent check is critical, and no legacy entry exceeds medium. Implemented in
`skills/audit-orchestrator/scripts/merge_agent_stage.py` and
`skills/entity-corroboration-agent/scripts/ingest_agent_findings.py`.

Every finding and baseline recommendation also exposes a stable `check_id`, `mechanism`,
`implementation_detail` and `expected_outcome` from common/check_catalog.py.
Merged findings include `check_ids` for all contributing rules. Per-report `id`
is unchanged. A finding whose severity the severity gate lowered (see
severity-rubric.md) carries `severity_gate`: `{"from", "to", "reason"}`. Every finding
carries `coverage`: `{"affected_pages", "pages_analyzed", "pages_discovered"}`; a finding moved
from confirmed to suspected because its confidence is below 0.7 carries `status_note`. The report
coverage block adds `audited_url` and `start_redirect` (null unless the start URL redirected to
another origin that was audited instead). The action object mirrors the two catalog action-detail fields.
Missing catalog entries are rejected on the public audit path. These fields do
not replace the observed root cause, evidence, status or affected-page list.
`affected_pages` is the full set within the crawl, never the evidence sample
(evidence lists at most 10 URLs per item). Every catalogued finding carries
`confidence_basis`: `{"method": "script_rule"}`, or for a value from
CALIBRATED_CONFIDENCE the dev-label basis (`labelled`, `tp`, `fp`,
`irrelevant`, `sites`, `evidence_class`, `dataset`, `dataset_sha256_prefix`,
`scored_on`, `labels_reviewed_by_person`, `base_value`, `method: heuristic`).
`top_actions` lists defects only; at most one, a confirmed defect, is marked
`start_here`, and a report may have none. Improvement opportunities
(`finding_type: proactive_improvement`) are never top actions.


Confidence: `confidence.score` is min(sample_representativeness,
observation_completeness, execution_penalty); identity_confidence is reported
beside it, not in it. sample_representativeness is
max(crawl_completeness, min(section_coverage, sample_size)), capped at 0.9
unless `coverage.discovery_reliable` is true: section_coverage is
sections with an analyzed page over sections discovered (a section is the first
path segment), and sample_size is pages analyzed over min(pages discovered, 30).
observation_completeness is (pages read in full + half of pages read in part)
over (pages analyzed + challenged, waiting-room, timed-out and failed fetches).
The score is a heuristic (`confidence.interpretation` says so), not a
calibrated probability. Coverage also reports `sections_discovered`,
`sections_analyzed` and `page_types_analyzed`.

Page counts: `pages_analyzed` counts 2xx pages kept for analysis that carry
visible text, structured data or scripts; a login, challenge or waiting-room
page, an error status, a failed fetch and a second URL redirecting to a page
already kept never count. Of those, `pages_fully_observed` were read in full and
`pages_partially_observed` were cut by the byte budget or the parser (what was
observed on them counts as evidence; nothing is concluded from what is missing
on them). `pages_redirect_duplicates` counts requests that redirected to a page
already kept, and `url_aliases` the requested URLs recorded as redirect aliases
(acquisition keeps the full map, so a link to any alias reaches the kept page).
Discovery: `sitemap_status`, `sitemap_url_entries`, `sitemap_candidates`,
`sitemap_partial` and `crawl_limit_reached` say how URLs were found.
`discovery_reliable` is true (a fully read sitemap and a crawl that fetched and
read every discovered URL), false (the crawl is known to have missed URLs) or
"unknown" (for example no sitemap); `coverage_scope` is none,
all_discovered_urls or sample. A finding never speaks for pages the crawl did
not discover.

The confidence block also carries `level` (high at 0.8 or more, medium at 0.5
or more, low below), `limiting_factor` (the component that set the score; on a
tie execution_penalty, then observation_completeness, then
sample_representativeness), `reasons` (one plain-English sentence per component) and
`explanation` (a short paragraph for a non-expert). These fields never change
the score. `coverage.identity_resolution` says how entity resolution went:
resolved, parent_sub_brand, multi_brand_conflict, conflicting_sameas,
no_organization_markup or not_completed. A report from an audit that did not
finish has score 0.0, method "no completed audit evidence" and an explanation
saying so.

Login pages: a fetched page is not analyzed only when it has a visible
password input, at most 1,500 characters of visible text, no entity structured
data (any JSON-LD or microdata type other than WebPage, WebSite, BreadcrumbList
and similar page boilerplate), and at least one of an auth path segment (login,
sign-in, wp-login.php and similar, matched as a whole segment so /author/ never
matches), a login title or H1, or a login form outside the header, nav and
footer. Any doubt keeps the page in scope. Coverage reports
`pages_out_of_scope`, `out_of_scope_pages` (up to 20 entries of `url`,
`final_url`, `reason` "login_page" and `signals`) and `start_url_out_of_scope`.
Login pages count toward the page cap and toward `pages_fetched_attempted` and
`pages_fetched_ok`, never toward `pages_analyzed`.

Action plan: every finding's and baseline recommendation's `suggested_action` also
carries `impact` (low, medium, high), `effort`, `quadrant` (quick_win,
major_project, fill_in, deprioritize), `quadrant_label` and `quick_win`.
Impact comes from severity, capped at medium for suspected items and low for
insufficient evidence, and raised from low to medium for a confirmed issue on
at least half of the analyzed pages. Medium and high impact count as valuable;
medium and high effort count as real work.

Top-level `top_actions` lists up to 5 defects in priority order with
`finding_id`, `title`, `summary`, `priority`, `impact`, `effort`, `quadrant`,
`quick_win` and `start_here` (the first quick win, or the first defect when
there is none). `quick_wins` lists up to 5 quick wins across findings and
recommendations.

Fix snippets: for no-jsonld, page-type-schema-gap:*, no-llms-txt,
llms-txt-invalid, missing-canonical, invalid-canonical, social-metadata,
social-url, twitter-card, location-markup-missing and contact-markup-missing,
`suggested_action.snippet`
holds ready-to-paste code built from observed facts (`format`, `placement`,
`code`, `filled_from`, `placeholders`, `note`). Values the audit did not
observe are REPLACE_WITH_... placeholders, all listed in `placeholders`.

No page analyzed: `execution_status` is `insufficient_evidence` and
`coverage.acquisition_failure` is `{"reason", "stage", "url", "http_status",
"detail"}` (plus `signals` for a bot challenge); it is null whenever a page was
analyzed. reason is one of dns_error, tls_error, timeout, connection_error,
unsupported_response, too_many_redirects, deadline, budget_exhausted,
cross_origin_redirect, robots_disallowed, robots_unavailable,
blocked_by_safety_policy, blocked_by_challenge, waiting_room, access_denied (401/403),
rate_limited (429), not_found, server_error, http_error, login_page, no_content
or not_fetched; stage is robots_txt or start_url. Only robots.txt, llms.txt,
sitemap and TLS checks are reported in that case.

Bot challenges: coverage reports `pages_challenged`, `challenged_pages` (up to
20 entries of `url`, `final_url`, `http_status` and `signals`) and
`crawl_stopped_by_challenge`. Challenged pages count toward
`pages_fetched_attempted`, never toward `pages_analyzed`.

Waiting rooms: a thin page whose title, first heading or opening text carries
queue wording ("You are now in line", "Routing to checkout"), or whose body
carries a queue provider's marker, together with interstitial evidence (a
refresh, almost no links, a queue status code or the same text as another
holding page) is a waiting room. A provider marker without queue wording needs
a refresh, a queue status or a repeated holding page. Coverage reports
`pages_waiting_room`, `waiting_room_pages` (up to 20 entries of `url`,
`final_url`, `http_status`, `reason` "waiting_room", `signals` and
`content_digest`) and `crawl_stopped_by_waiting_room`; the crawl stops at the
same thresholds as for challenges. Waiting rooms are never retried, rendered or
waited out, and never analyzed as content.

Transport diagnostics: coverage includes transport_diagnostics, transport_environment,
robots_attempt_outcomes and robots_permission. Failed robots acquisition means
permission unknown, zero inferred blocks and degraded execution. Exact TLS
verification details and recovered attempt outcomes are retained separately from
site findings. An HTTP status is not a TLS error.

## Evidence/outcome contract 1.1

The report stores `packet_sha256` for the final evidence packet. The packet and
outcome envelope use schema 1.1 and bind audit, snapshot and packet digests. The
project digest encoding is sorted compact UTF-8 JSON (ensure_ascii=False,
allow_nan=False), excluding only the packet's top-level packet_sha256. Timing
and focused evidence are finalized before hashing. The snapshot digest includes
retained JSON-LD, opening/section observations, anchors and observation states,
as well as page identity/status/title and visible-text digests; no omitted raw
bytes are covered.

`coverage.evidence_packet_bytes` holds the exact emitted UTF-8 byte count with
digest and newline; it is not stored inside the packet. `evidence_packet_ready`
and optional `evidence_packet_write_failed` distinguish assessment eligibility
and export failures. Non-ready exports carry the current audit and actual reason.

`focus.ag01` binds the selected body opening, exact fragment ID, E page reference,
source kind, completeness, selection reason and limitations. Page generic and
focused text share the 4,000-character allowance. Every retained C parent, I
association and identity/focus source reference resolves after fitting.

Old schema 1.0 assessments require regenerated export/assessment. Legacy arrays
remain partial and clear no missing-capability limits. Per-check semantic fields
and scope are described in entity-corroboration-agent/SKILL.md.
