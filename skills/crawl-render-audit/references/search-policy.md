# Search policy scope — checked 13 October 2026

Google Search ignores llms.txt for visibility/ranking, including its generative
Search features. Absence creates no Google defect or penalty; presence proves no
Google benefit. Acquisition keeps bounded observations. A deliberately supplied
artifact can still have broken links or format issues for its configured readers.
Identify an actual non-Google consumer before recommending optional publication;
for example, the llms.txt proposal documents the `server-llm-txt` MCP retrieval integration.
This is an optional retrieval/context workflow, not universal assistant support.

FAQ rich results stopped appearing in Google Search on 7 May 2026. Missing
FAQPage is no website defect. Useful audience Q&A and generic FAQPage parsing
remain supported; malformed JSON or supported content mismatches still get their
ordinary diagnoses. Do not invent questions or recommend markup for the retired
feature. HowTo rich results are also retired. Optional structured export advice
must identify its content reuse workflow, with unmeasured benefits.

Observed access, readable facts and repairs do not prove visibility, indexing,
ranking, citations or conversions. Those outcomes require separate measurements.
